《失落之路与其他作品》（The Lost Road and Other Writings）是《中洲历史》十二卷的第五卷。本书共分为三个部分：第一部分主要讲述了几个早期版本的《努门诺尔沦亡史》和一份未完成的时间旅行作品《失落之路》，世界变圆和通往西方的笔直航道首次出现；第二部分主要讲述了晚期的一些文稿和相关资料，包括晚期的《维林诺编年史》、《贝烈瑞安德编年史》、《爱努大乐章》、《精灵宝钻征战史》和故事人物与情节的设定说明。第三部分是一则有关复杂的语言系统及中洲方言的论文，含一份超长的精灵语语源词汇表。以及其英文版最早于1987年8月27日由乔治·艾伦与昂温出版社在英国出版发行，于1987年11月30日由霍顿·米夫林出版公司在美国出版发行。

As friends and fellow members of the literary circle known as The Inklings, J.R.R. Tolkien and C.S. Lewis embarked on a challenge. Lewis was to write on "space-travel" and Tolkien on "time-travel." Lewis's novel Out of the Silent Planet became the first book of a science finction trilogy. Tolkien's unfinished story "The Lost Road" chronicles the original destruction of Numenor, a pivotal event of the Second Age of Middle-earth. In this intriguing volume, Christopher Tolkien traces the vivid history of Middle-earth,bringing the land——its topography and ever-clashing forces——to the state readers recognize from The Lord of the Rings. Entertaining and informative, The Lost Road and Other Writings shares fresh insights into the evolution of one of the world's most enduring fantasies.
