切奥武甫（Céolwulf），是著名水手艾尔夫威奈的好友。1

特列奥威奈在国王座前朗诵了一首诗歌，但在《概念社文集》的文稿中却并非如此，文稿这样写道：

At that one of the Marchers leaped to his feet and got leave to speak. Even before I had found a seat beside Tréowine, whom I espied far down the hall, the fellow had a foot on the step and had begun. He had a good voice, if a strange way with his words. Céolwulf, as I heard later, was his name, and he ciaimed to come of the blood of their kings that sat at Tamworth of old. His verse was in the old style ...

—— 概念社文集之二，注释103
